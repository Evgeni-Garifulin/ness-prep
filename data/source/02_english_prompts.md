# 2. English interview prompts

1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Why are you interested in Exness?
3. Describe your current project.
4. What was your biggest technical challenge?
5. Describe a complex frontend architecture you worked on.
6. How do you usually approach debugging?
7. Tell me about a time you had a conflict with a teammate.
8. Tell me about a time you disagreed with a product decision.
9. How do you handle unclear requirements?
10. How do you explain technical trade-offs to non-technical people?
11. What does ownership mean to you?
12. How do you mentor other engineers?
13. What kind of team environment helps you perform best?
14. What are your salary expectations?
15. Why are you leaving your current role?
16. What are your strengths as a frontend engineer?
17. What are your weaknesses?
18. What would you improve in your current product?
19. What frontend trends do you consider overhyped?
20. What questions do you have for us?
